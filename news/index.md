# Changelog

## nycosm 0.0.1.9000

- Initial release.

- [`nyc_osm_points()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm.md),
  [`nyc_osm_lines()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm.md),
  [`nyc_osm_buildings()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm.md),
  [`nyc_osm_areas()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm.md),
  and
  [`nyc_osm_routes()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm.md)
  give lazy access to OpenStreetMap features for New York City
  (Geofabrik extract of 2026-10-18), stored as GeoParquet in EPSG:2263,
  with full tags.

- [`nyc_osm_path()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm_path.md),
  [`nyc_osm_to_sf()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm_to_sf.md),
  and
  [`nyc_osm_add_tags()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycosm/reference/nyc_osm_add_tags.md)
  helpers.
