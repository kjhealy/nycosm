# nycosm 0.0.1.9000

* Initial release.

* `nyc_osm_points()`, `nyc_osm_lines()`, `nyc_osm_buildings()`,
  `nyc_osm_areas()`, and `nyc_osm_routes()` give lazy access to
  OpenStreetMap features for New York City (Geofabrik extract of
  2026-10-18), stored as GeoParquet in EPSG:2263, with full tags.

* `nyc_osm_path()`, `nyc_osm_to_sf()`, and `nyc_osm_add_tags()` helpers.
